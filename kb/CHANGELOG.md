## 2026-09-30 — Fixed Tighnari doing almost no damage in a custom team

- User reported Tighnari dealing significantly less damage than Fischl/Kuki Shinobu in a custom Yaoyao (Deepwood Memories) + Fischl + Kuki Shinobu + Tighnari team, and asked to double-check the calculation. Two real bugs found and fixed:
  1. **tighnari.json (patch)**: when I added his Charged Attack ("Wreath Arrow") in the previous session, I never updated his `usualCombo` — it still repeated plain physical Normal Attacks (the bulk-import default), which get zero benefit from Quicken/Spread since they're not Dendro. Fixed: `usualCombo` now casts Skill, Burst, then repeats Charged Attack (his actual on-field loop).
  2. **`src/kb/custom.ts` (`buildCustomRotation`), engine bug**: the custom-team rotation builder only ever expanded `repeat` for the `'normal'` action — any other action (Charged Attack, Skill, an extraAction) with `repeat: 'untilRotationEnd'` silently fired once and stopped, no matter what a character's `usualCombo` said. This affected every custom team built through the app UI, not just Tighnari's, though he's the first hand-touched character in this KB whose on-field loop isn't Normal Attacks. Fixed: `repeat` now applies uniformly to any action.
  - Re-simulated the user's exact team after both fixes: Tighnari went from 1,086 → 5,731 relaxed DPS (now clearly the team's top damage dealer, as expected for the Aggravate driver), Fischl/Kuki-Shinobu unchanged at ~1,000–1,500 each.
  - New regression test: `tests/teams.test.ts` ("repeats a non-normal action... for the rest of the rotation, not just once").

## 2026-09-29 — Bulk-import bug: "Absorption" (shield) labels imported as damage hits

- User asked for a tier-list sanity check ("do teams that should rank well actually rank well?") across ≥5 team comps built from their roster. Simulating a Noelle mono-Geo team (Noelle/Gorou/Kachina/Ningguang, all baseline) put it far above every other team in the roster, including the known-top-tier Varesa Overloaded team (42,332 vs 18,011 relaxed DPS) — implausible for a F2P mono-Geo comp.
- Root cause: `scripts/kb-import/bulk-character.ts`'s `NOT_DAMAGE` regex excluded genshin-db labels containing `absorb`, but Noelle's Breastplate (skill) shield-capacity label is genshin-db's `"DMG Absorption"` — `absorb` (ends in `b`) does not match `Absorption` (continues `...sorp...`), so the filter missed it. The shield's raw `769.7–2431%` DEF value (a shield HP amount, not a damage multiplier) was imported as a third "hit" on Noelle's skill, dealing 770–2400× her DEF as fake damage on every cast.
- Fix: `NOT_DAMAGE` now matches `absor` (covers `absorb`, `absorbed`, `absorption`, …). Re-ran the bulk importer for the 5 characters whose baseline records had a hit with mv > 15 (a quick audit across all 429 records): `noelle` (the bug above, now `medium`, 7/7 multiplier tables — down from a bogus 3rd hit), `illuga`, `ineffa`, `nefer`, `kinich` (none owned by the reporting user; their high mv values were re-checked against genshin-db's labels afterwards and are genuine large single-hit burst/skill multipliers, not further instances of this bug).
- Side effect: re-running the bulk importer for these 5 also re-fetched their recommended main stats; KeqingMains is currently blocked from the cloud environment (see `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`), so all 5 lost their previously-sourced KeqingMains main-stat rows and fell back to the generic template. Re-run `/kb-add-character` for these 5 locally (where KeqingMains is reachable) to restore sourced main stats.
- Broader finding (not a bug, a modelling-completeness gap): team DPS ranking currently tracks how many of the 4 members are hand-written vs baseline much more than real-world team strength. A team whose carry/support depends on unmodelled passives or constellations (e.g. Noelle's kit and Gorou's DEF/RES buff, both still baseline) will rank far below its real tier, while a team built entirely from hand-written kits (Varesa Overloaded, the Varka teams) tracks real tier expectations reasonably well. See docs/PROGRESS_LOG.md for the full 7-team comparison.

## 2026-09-27 — Ascension CRIT stats no longer include the base CRIT
- 42 characters with a CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG ascension stat were stored with genshin-db's `specialized` value, which includes the base 5% CRIT Rate / 50% CRIT DMG that the engine adds itself. Stored values corrected: CRIT Rate 0.242 → 0.192, CRIT DMG 0.884 → 0.384 (e.g. Hu Tao, Yelan, Ayaka, Neuvillette, Furina, Odette). Importers now subtract the base (`ascensionValue` in scripts/kb-import/lib.ts).
- Effect: those characters' CRIT was overstated by 5 pts CR or 50 pts CD. Golden value for hu-tao-double-hydro-zhongli re-recorded (Relaxed 31,063 → 28,086 DPS). raiden-national unchanged.
